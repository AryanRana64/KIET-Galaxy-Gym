About Us

Welcome to KIET Galaxy Gym. We provide a clean, fully equipped space designed to help you reach your fitness goals, whether you want to build muscle, lose weight, or stay active.


Our gym is equipped with state-of-the-art machines, free weights, and cardio equipment. Our certified trainers are here to guide you through personalized workout plans and ensure you get the most out of your fitness journey.


Our gym is only available to the Hostel Students. Students can access the gym during designated hours.
